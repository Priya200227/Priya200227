## What I've built

### 📉 Subscription Revenue Leakage & Churn Intelligence
Built a churn model (Scikit-learn, SMOTE for class imbalance) reaching **ROC-AUC 0.93 / Recall 0.86**, then quantified **₹96M+ in recurring revenue at risk** and segmented it by churn probability in a 3-page executive Power BI dashboard.
`MySQL` `Python` `Scikit-learn` `Power BI`
**[Repo →](https://github.com/Priya200227/subscription-revenue-leakage-churn-intelligence-system)**

### 🚚 Seller Fulfillment Delay & Revenue Intelligence
Analyzed 90K+ Olist e-commerce orders and built a composite Fulfillment Risk Index for SLA monitoring. Statistically confirmed the delay-satisfaction link (t=85.15, p<0.0001) — on-time orders average a 4.29 review score vs. 2.60 for late ones. Delay prediction model: MAE 5.87 days.
`MySQL` `Python` `SciPy` `Power BI`
**[Repo →](https://github.com/Priya200227/seller-fulfillment-delay-revenue-impact-intelligence)**

### 📊 Revenue Decline & Return Risk Analytics
RFM-segmented 20K+ transactions in BigQuery to find that ~78% of revenue comes from loyal customers, while Footwear carries the highest return risk at 26%. Published live on Tableau Public.
`BigQuery` `Python` `Tableau`
**[Repo →](https://github.com/Priya200227/revenue-decline-return-optimization-analysis)**
